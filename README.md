# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis

An interactive **HR Analytics and Employee Attrition Analysis** project built using **Power BI, SQL, DAX, and employee workforce data**.

The project analyzes employee demographics, job roles, departments, compensation, job satisfaction, business travel, overtime, and tenure to identify patterns associated with employee attrition and support data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and create workforce planning challenges.

This project aims to answer key HR questions such as:

- How many employees are leaving the organization?
- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments experience higher attrition?
- Which job roles are more affected by employee turnover?
- How does attrition vary across age groups and gender?
- Is overtime associated with higher attrition?
- How does business travel relate to employee turnover?
- What role does job satisfaction play in workforce retention?
- How does employee education and tenure relate to attrition?

---

## 📌 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition
- Calculate key HR KPIs
- Identify department-wise attrition patterns
- Analyze attrition by job role and job level
- Examine age-group and gender patterns
- Analyze the relationship between overtime and attrition
- Investigate business travel patterns
- Analyze job satisfaction across job roles
- Examine education and education-field patterns
- Identify workforce trends that can support retention strategies

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The dashboard helps HR teams identify important workforce patterns, including:

- Employee attrition varies significantly across departments and job roles.
- Workforce composition differs across age groups.
- Overtime can be examined as an important factor associated with employee attrition.
- Business travel patterns provide another perspective on employee turnover.
- Job satisfaction varies across different job roles.
- Employee education fields show differences in attrition levels.
- Attrition patterns can be further explored by combining demographic and employment characteristics.

> The dashboard is designed to help HR teams move from descriptive reporting toward data-driven workforce analysis.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, HR teams can consider:

### 1. Strengthen Retention Strategies
Identify employee segments with relatively higher attrition and develop targeted retention initiatives.

### 2. Monitor High-Risk Job Roles
Review job roles with higher employee turnover to understand workload, compensation, career growth, and satisfaction factors.

### 3. Address Overtime Concerns
Monitor employees frequently working overtime and evaluate workload distribution and employee well-being.

### 4. Improve Employee Engagement
Use job satisfaction insights to identify areas where employee engagement initiatives may be required.

### 5. Support Career Development
Provide training, internal mobility, and career-growth opportunities to improve employee retention.

### 6. Use Data for Workforce Planning
Combine attrition, tenure, age, department, and job-role information to support proactive workforce planning.
